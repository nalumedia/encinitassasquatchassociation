The Encinitas Sasquatch Association

Believes 

We celebrate Bigfoot’s love of Encinitas, California. Over the years there have been several well-documented sightings of the elusive sasquatch as they make their way south for the winter. Some say that they have heard bigfoot speak of how the sand, the sea, and the great vibe Encinitas has makes it a favorite stop for all magical creatures on their journeys.
