We are the Encinitas Sasquatch Association

Founded in 2016, our mission is to inspire people to believe. We believe that positivity and an open mind can change the world for good. We celebrate Bigfoot’s love of Encinitas, California. Over the years there have been several well-documented sightings of the elusive sasquatch as they make their way south for the winter. Some say that they have heard bigfoot speak of how the sand, the sea, and the great vibe Encinitas has makes it a favorite stop for all magical creatures on their journeys.

We unite and inspire people who believe that there is something out there.

Questions from the trail

🧐 What is the Encinitas Sasquatch Association?

Founded in 2016, our mission is to inspire people to believe. We believe that positivity and an open mind can change the world for good.

🏖️ Why does Sasquatch have to do with Encinitas?

We believe that Encinitas (America’s finest town) is Bigfoot’s favorite stop on his annual migration south for the winter. Some say Bigfoot is attracted to the sand, the surf, and the great vibes in Encinitas.

👣 Does Bigfoot exist?

The absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. You never know what you will find along the way when you set out to do something awesome.

🌱 What’s up with the lima beans?

We believe that it may have been the delicious scent of the lima beans growing in the fields of Encinitas that may have brought a hungry squatch to Encinitas for the first time. The lima bean is the unsung hero of classic Encinitas. After its introduction in 1905 there were 25k pounds of lima beans harvested here each day. Grow your own, if you want to test the theory.

🤚 How do I join the ESA?

We welcome anyone and everyone who believes in something. Fill out the membership form and you’ll get a welcome letter from the ESA crew.
